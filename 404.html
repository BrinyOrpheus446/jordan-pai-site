About

A future doctor drawn to the places care rarely reaches.

I study Global Health at Georgetown University (class of '27) and I'm a Global Health Institute Fellow. I spent 14 weeks in the Pilbara in Western Australia, based between Karratha and Roebourne on Ngarluma Country, researching rural health placements with the Western Australian Centre for Rural Health and volunteering with local community and youth services.

That time showed me how much a good placement, a trusted clinician or a clear answer can change for a remote community. Everything I make now aims at that: better-prepared clinicians, and more people who choose medicine and go where they're needed.

Rural placement research

Researcher

Question
What makes a rural placement good, and does it change whether students want to practise rurally?
My role
Mapped the student survey onto the first national quality framework and ran the analysis (2019–2025, n = 1,293).
Finding
Every quality measure was linked to stronger rural intent. Learning opportunities and valued autonomy led, at about 1.8 times the odds per SD. Preliminary results, not yet peer reviewed.

I don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and I don't buy or receive data about you from anyone else.

Sign-ups and visit counts are stored in the United States, in a private Google Sheet (Google) or, if you're viewing the site on Claude, with Anthropic. Fonts load from Google Fonts and a small scrolling library loads from jsDelivr, so those services receive your IP address when the page loads. Links to Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it, or two years after we last spoke, whichever comes first.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from updates at any time by replying "unsubscribe".
  • Change your visit-count choice with the buttons below at any time.
  • Residents of some US states, such as California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia, Colorado and Connecticut, have specific rights to know, correct and delete personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. I don't sell or share personal information, and I honor these requests for everyone.
